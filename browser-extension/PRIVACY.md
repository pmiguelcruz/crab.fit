# Crab Fit browser extension privacy

The extension saves the Crab Fit website address you configure and the poll
links and titles you choose to keep. It stores them in this browser profile.
You can remove a poll from the list or clear the extension's stored data by
uninstalling it.

When you open the toolbar popup, the extension can read the active tab's
address and title to offer **Save this poll** on your configured Crab Fit
website. It saves that information only when you choose to save the poll.
**Copy link** writes the saved link to your clipboard. The extension does
not collect analytics or send its saved poll list to a server.

Opening or creating a poll opens the chosen Crab Fit website in a browser
tab. That website handles the poll and submitted availability under its own
privacy policy.
